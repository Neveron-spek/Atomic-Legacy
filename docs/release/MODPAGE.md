# Atomic Legacy — HBM's Nuclear Tech Port

**Summary (one line):** Unofficial port of HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod (1.7.10) to Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge.

---

**Atomic Legacy** brings the classic **HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod** — reactors, radiation, nuclear weapons, chemistry, heavy industry — to **Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge**.

The aim is a *faithful* port of the 1.7.10 mod: original textures, models, sounds, recipes, numbers and behaviour. Changes are limited to what the new platform requires, plus a short list of documented fixes.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Most content is in and passes automated checks, but in-game testing has only started. Play in a separate world and keep backups. Bug reports are very welcome.

## Features
- Radiation and contamination: chunk radiation, hazardous items, crater biomes, Geiger counters, protective gear.
- Nuclear and conventional weapons, missiles and launch pads, fallout and craters.
- Fission and fusion: research and breeding reactors, RBMK, PWR, ICF, ZIRNOX, Chicago Pile.
- Industry: presses, assemblers, chemical plants, refineries, centrifuges, heat systems, power networks.
- Mobs, structures and world generation of the original mod.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250+, Java 21.
- Optional: JEI (recipes), Create, Immersive Engineering, Applied Energistics 2, TACZ.
