## Description

# ☢ Atomic Legacy
**An unofficial port of HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod (Minecraft 1.7.10, by HbmMods) to Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge.**

Atomic Legacy turns Minecraft into a nuclear-age industrial survival game. You mine radioactive ores, build power grids and factories, run reactors that can melt down, protect yourself from radiation, and — if you want — build weapons up to the Tsar Bomba. It keeps the original mod's textures, models, sounds, recipes and numbers, so it plays like the HBM's NTM you remember.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Content is complete and passes automated checks, but in-game testing has only just started. Play in a separate world and keep backups.

---

## Radiation — the core survival threat
- **Radioactive materials:** uranium, plutonium, thorium, schrabidium, nuclear waste and spent fuel emit radiation while in your inventory or placed nearby. Uranium ore releases **radon gas** when mined.
- **Chunk radiation:** reactors, leaks, waste and fallout contaminate the area. Radiation spreads between chunks and slowly decays.
- **Radiation sickness:** your dose builds up over time and causes weakness, nausea, blindness and finally death. Every mob is affected, and some mutate.
- **Measuring it:** Geiger counters (item and block) show chunk radiation, the dose you receive per second, your total dose and your protection. Dosimeters give a quick reading.
- **Protecting yourself:** hazmat suits, gas masks with replaceable filters, power armor and anti-radiation medicine (Rad-X, RadAway) reduce or remove radiation.

**What it means for you:** radioactive materials are valuable but dangerous to carry and store. You plan shielding, protective gear and storage, and every accident leaves a contaminated area behind.

## Nuclear power
- **Research reactor, breeding reactor, ZIRNOX, PWR, RBMK** (with its full control console, rods and crane), **Chicago Pile, Watz** and **ICF laser fusion**.
- Reactors need fuel, cooling, coolant and control. If you mismanage heat, they **melt down or explode** and leave debris and radiation.
- **Fuel cycle:** enrichment (gas centrifuges, SILEX), fuel rods and pellets, spent fuel reprocessing (PUREX) and waste handling.

**What it means for you:** large amounts of power, earned by building and running a reactor carefully. Getting it wrong has real consequences for your base.

## Industry and energy
- **Power (HE) network:** generators (combustion, diesel, steam turbines, RTG and more), cables, batteries and capacitors.
- **Fluid network:** pipes, barrels and tanks for oil, gases, steam, coolants and dozens of chemicals.
- **Machines:** presses, assemblers and precision assemblers, chemical plants and factories, oil refineries and cracking, centrifuges, crucibles and foundry casting, blast furnace, shredder, heat exchangers and boilers. Machines run on recipe templates and support upgrades.
- **Logistics:** conveyors, cranes (inserters, extractors, routers) and pneumatic tubes to automate whole production lines.

**What it means for you:** a deep progression from coal and a hand press to automated factories, oil processing and nuclear fuel production.

## Weapons and explosives
- **Nuclear bombs:** Gadget, Little Boy, Fat Man, Ivy Mike, Tsar Bomba and more. They create mushroom clouds, shockwaves, **craters with their own biomes** and **fallout** that turns terrain into scorched, irradiated ground.
- **Missiles and launch pads**, a custom missile builder, airstrikes and supply drops.
- **Firearms:** the original weapon system with many guns, ammunition types, magazines and modifications, plus grenades, mines and charges.

**What it means for you:** endgame destructive power with real, lasting effects on the world. Plan where you test your bombs.

## Armor and equipment
- **Power armor** (T-51, RPA, NCRPA and more), **hazmat** and **HEV** suits, all rendered as 3D models on the player.
- Jetpacks, wings, flashlights and armor mods that add protection, utility and resistances.
- Medicine, tools and utility items for dangerous environments.

## World and mobs
- **New ores**, **bedrock ores** that can be mined endlessly with drills, **oil deposits**, meteorites and radioactive deposits.
- **Structures** to explore and loot: laboratories, radio stations, military bases, oil rigs, an arctic station, bunkers and the beached Soyuz capsule.
- **Mobs and bosses:** glyphids and their hives, nuclear and other special creepers, crabs, the RAD beast, FBI agents, Mask Man, the UFO, the hunter chopper and the BOT Prime worm.

**What it means for you:** new reasons to explore, new resources to fight over, and threats that scale with how much you industrialise.

## Requirements
- Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.250 or newer**, Java **21**
- Recommended: **JEI** for recipes of every machine
- Optional integrations: Create, Immersive Engineering, Applied Energistics 2, TACZ

## Notes
- Works in singleplayer and on dedicated servers. Install the same file on the server and on every client.
- Start a **new world**: builds released before the rename (mod id `reinhardtshbm`) are not compatible.
