## Description

# ☢ Atomic Legacy
### HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod, brought to Minecraft 1.21.1

**Atomic Legacy** is an unofficial port of the classic **HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod** (1.7.10, by HbmMods) to **Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge**.

The goal is simple: **the original mod, not a reimagining.** Original textures, models, sounds, recipes, numbers and behaviour — changed only where the new platform forces it, plus a short, documented list of fixes.

> ⚠️ **Alpha build.** Almost all content is in and passes a large set of automated checks, but in-game testing has only just started. Play in a separate world, keep backups — and please report bugs!

## What's inside
- **Radiation** — contaminated chunks, radioactive items and ores, radon, crater biomes after a nuclear blast, Geiger counters, dosimeters, hazmat suits and gas masks.
- **Nuclear power** — research and breeding reactors, RBMK with its control console, PWR, ZIRNOX, Chicago Pile, Watz, ICF lasers, fuel reprocessing.
- **Weapons of mass destruction** — from small charges to the Tsar Bomba: mushroom clouds, fallout, craters, missiles and launch pads.
- **Heavy industry** — presses, assemblers, chemical plants, refineries, centrifuges, crucibles and foundries, heat exchangers, turbines, power and fluid networks.
- **Guns and armor** — the original firearm system, power armor, jetpacks and suits rendered on the player.
- **World** — new ores, bedrock ores, oil deposits, structures, meteorites, glyphid hives, the UFO, the chopper, BOT Prime and more.

## Requirements
- Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.250+**, Java **21**
- Recommended: **JEI** (recipes for every machine)
- Optional integrations: Create, Immersive Engineering, Applied Energistics 2, TACZ

## Good to know
- This is a **new world** mod — builds released before the rename (mod id `reinhardtshbm`) are not compatible.
- Runs on dedicated servers; install the same jar on server and clients.
